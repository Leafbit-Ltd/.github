Install

  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. Open your mail client's signature settings.
  3. Create or edit a signature, then paste the copied signature into the editor.
  4. Save it and send a test email to confirm the logo, email link, and website link work.

For Apple Mail, use Mail > Settings > Signatures, then paste into the signature preview area.
